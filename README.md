**Lebanon's Institutions and Commercial Activity by District**
**Project Overview** 
This project investigates business activity throughout 18 districts in Lebanon. 
The app lets users to look at trends and variations between districts' various forms of commercial activity , and the sizes of institutions.
The first visualization is a bubble chat that shows the distribution of small, medim and large insitutions across districts, with each bubble size indicating the number of instiutions.
The second visualization is a stacked bar chart which represents the composition of business activity among districts.

**Data Set**
The data set contains information on business establishments and service operation in various towns, governorates and districts of Lebanon. 
I grouped the data by district in order to examine and focus on institution sizes and business activity. 

***Important Note: Even though the data does not record all 26 districts , I still chose to display the sizes by district to reduce the clutter as a result of choosing towns, or the lack of depth from choosing governorates. 
However, this is a limitation and it is important to note that if Beirut and Chouf trade data was recorded, it would have impacted the dominating categories.***
